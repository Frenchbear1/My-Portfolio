Takeoff

172S POH limits: 2,550 lb max takeoff; CG 35.0–47.3 in (forward limit rises to 41.0 in at 2,550 lb). Utility category (spins approved): 2,200 lb, CG 35.0–40.5 in, nobody in back, no baggage. Arms in inches aft of the firewall. Use your airplane's own empty weight and arm.
