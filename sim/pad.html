Flight

Yoke: hold the phone sideways. Turn it like a steering wheel to roll. Tip the top edge toward you to pull back (nose up) and away from you to push. Hold two fingers on the gauges, or press CENTER TILT, to make the current position neutral. With tilt off, drag on the gauges to move the yoke.

Engine start: MASTER (BAT) on, FUEL SHUTOFF in, MIXTURE rich, throttle cracked, then tap the key to BOTH and press and hold it for START. Pulling the mixture to idle cut-off, turning the key OFF, pulling the fuel shutoff or running a tank dry stops the engine.

Turn your phone sideways (landscape) to fly.

Skyhawk cockpit

Hold the phone in landscape like a yoke, then tap start. You will be asked to allow motion sensors so tilting can fly the airplane.
